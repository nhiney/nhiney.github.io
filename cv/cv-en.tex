\cvHeading
  {Smart Medical Booking App}
  {Feb 2026 -- May 2026}
  {Team Lead · Business Analyst \& Core Developer · 4-member team}

\cvProblem{Problem: Appointment scheduling lacks real-time conflict prevention and is hard to use for elderly patients — causing double-bookings and low self-service adoption.}

\begin{cvBullets}
  \item Ran \textbf{user research} on the booking journey and turned the findings into \textbf{Figma wireframes and user flows} the team built from; surfaced voice interaction as an accessibility need, driving a \textbf{voice-assisted booking} feature.
  \item Defined \textbf{three actors — Patient, Doctor, Administrator} — each with a distinct permission scope and flow, settling access rules before implementation rather than patching them afterwards.
  \item Identified \textbf{double-booking} as the critical business rule (two patients must never hold the same slot), specified the rule, then \textbf{implemented} its enforcement as an atomic \textbf{Firebase Transaction} on the slot record.
  \item \textbf{Wrote the majority of the codebase} as core developer: the Flutter/Dart client against Firestore, role-based access for the three actors, the voice-assisted booking flow and Google Maps clinic search.
  \item Led the \textbf{4-member team} — decomposed features into sprint tasks and tracked delivery — then \textbf{authored and executed 100+ manual test scenarios} on physical devices to validate the booking, scheduling and permission flows.
\end{cvBullets}
